Drying a home is only half the job. Even with normal moisture readings, you are left with opened walls, flooring that is gone and exposed subfloor. Water damage repair is the rebuild. In Missouri City, we put back the materials that were lost and match them to the originals, turning a work zone into a normal room again.

Repair work covers a wide range. Smaller projects might involve only paint, fresh baseboard and a short section of drywall. At the bigger end, expect doors, trim, cabinet boxes, hardwood or laminate flooring, subfloor, full drywall sheets and insulation. We replace materials like for like where possible, and we tell you plainly when an exact match is not available so you can choose.

We do not close anything in while it is still damp. Before any new material goes on, we confirm with a moisture meter that framing and subfloor are at normal levels. Building over damp wood is how mold ends up behind a freshly painted wall. You also receive a written scope and schedule ahead of the work, which spells out what gets rebuilt and when.

Signs It Is Time for Water Damage Repair

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Where water ran down a wall, the paint has blistered, peeled or turned brown.
  • Walking over the formerly wet area, you feel soft spots in the floor.
  • Cabinet bases or side panels are swollen, soft or peeling at the bottom.
  • Laminate or vinyl plank edges are swollen, curled or separating at the seams.
  • Wood floor boards stayed raised, cupped or crowned even once the moisture was gone.
  • A leak overhead left the ceiling drywall cracked, sagging or falling apart.
  • Baseboards and door casings are warped or no longer sit flat against the wall.

Water Damage Repair, Step by Step

Our systematic approach delivers quality results on every project. Here's how we handle water damage repair from start to finish:

1

Verify Dryness First

Final moisture readings are taken on the subfloor, framing and any drywall left in place. Only when each material reads normal does rebuilding start, which keeps damp spots from being sealed inside.

2

Put the Scope in Writing

We hand you a list, room by room, covering what gets replaced, planned materials and the likely schedule. If a claim is open, we line the scope up with the adjuster.

3

Reconstruct and Install

We refill open cavities with insulation, hang, tape and finish drywall, and repair the subfloor. Flooring, cabinets and trim are then installed in the order that protects the finished surfaces.

4

Final Finishes and Walkthrough

Texture is matched, walls are primed and painted, and baseboards, doors and hardware go back on. Then we walk every room with you and fix anything on the list before we call the job complete.

Water Damage Repair Prevention Tips

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Hold off on heavy traffic and wet cleaning until new adhesives, caulk and paint have cured.
  • Caulk around tubs, showers and sinks yearly so water stays out of the wall.
  • Laundry rooms, bathrooms and kitchens should be rebuilt with moisture resistant wallboard and proper backer board.
  • Rebuilt bathroom walls last longer when the exhaust fan runs through and after each shower.
  • Before a contractor closes a floor or wall, request the final moisture numbers.
  • Save spare tile and flooring, plus paint color details, for matching later repairs.
